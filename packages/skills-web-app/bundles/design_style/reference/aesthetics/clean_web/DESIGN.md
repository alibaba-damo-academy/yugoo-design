# clean_web — 简洁品牌网页

Use for public product sites, service introductions, documentation portals, and
content-led websites when the brand is understated and no expressive campaign
direction is requested.

## Vocabulary

- Use a clear reading order, meaningful page sections, and one primary action.
  Whitespace is structural rather than a substitute for content.
- A white surface is valid when it creates the clearest content hierarchy; avoid
  turning every section into a pale coloured card.
- Keep brand colour concentrated in actions, selected states, and a small number
  of identity-bearing details.

## Finish standard

- Let one product promise or concrete object lead the reading order; a wide hero
  with a generic slogan and three identical cards is not a finished composition.
- Use a deliberate content measure, two or three distinct vertical rhythms, and
  one meaningful supporting visual or proof point when the offer needs it.
  Whitespace should clarify the offer, not leave the page under-designed.
- Keep the surface quiet, but make type, rules, image cropping, and CTA alignment
  exact. Avoid pale-blue section bands repeated merely to make the page feel
  designed.

## Imagery

When the offer is visual, use one or two real, product-specific images with a
clear purpose. Generate images for the product itself; source real scenarios
when context is the subject; persist and verify every image used by the preview.
