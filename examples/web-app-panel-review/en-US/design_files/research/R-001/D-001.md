## Details and recovery

A customer may append one sentence in chat. The designer must first determine whether it belongs to the agreed scope. Length or apparent simplicity does not establish impact. The lead needs the evidence and a way to request one specific missing fact instead of requiring every field again.

When an attachment is inaccessible, retain its source location and the explanation already entered. A network failure, an uncertain save and a business decline are different states and need different feedback. Notification failure must not reverse a saved decision.
